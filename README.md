## How does IPTV on Chromecast compare with casting from your phone?

This is where the terminology causes a little chaos.

**Casting** means your phone or another device sends supported media to the television. Google documents this as a separate Google Cast workflow.

With **Chromecast with Google TV**, you can instead install apps directly on the streaming device. That gives you a more traditional TV experience: grab the remote, open the IPTV player and watch without keeping the phone involved.

For regular IPTV use, that distinction matters. If you plan to watch for a couple of hours on the sofa, having everything on the television is usually less fiddly than juggling a phone, casting session and three percent battery anxiety.
